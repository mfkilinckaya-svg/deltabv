Methods and built-in checks

Symmetric RCV (Harris & Yasaka; Fraser)

RCV = √2 · Z · √(CVA² + CVI²)

Asymmetric, log-normal RCV (Fokkema et al.)

σ = √( ln(1+CVA²) + ln(1+CVI²) ) (CVs as fractions) RCV↑ = exp( +√2 · Z · σ ) − 1 RCV↓ = exp( −√2 · Z · σ ) − 1

Uncertainty from the CVI estimate

RCV is recomputed at the lower and upper CI limits of CVI. A wide range means the delta-check limit itself is uncertain and the CVI estimate deserves scrutiny before it goes into the LIS.

Personalized reference interval (Coşkun et al. 2021)

HSP = mean of n previous results TVset = Z · √((n+1)/n) · √(CVI² + CVA²) prRI = HSP ± HSP · TVset / 100

Within-person model (Coskun et al. 2022)

SD_TV = SD of the n previous results prRI = HSP ± t(1−α/2, n−1) · SD_TV · √((n+1)/n)

Needs at least five steady-state results; the patient's own SD replaces the population CVI and CVA.

With n = 1 and a two-sided Z, TVset equals the symmetric RCV. Coşkun et al. recommend at least three previous steady-state results.

Checks against published values

Published values are compared within ±0.05 (±0.1 µmol/L for patient B, where the source rounds SD and half-width) to allow for rounding in the source.
